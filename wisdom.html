Wisdom, Safety, and Privacy

In some places, following Jesus is costly. We want to help you walk wisely and to protect one another. Nothing here should put you, or those around you, at risk. Seek the Lord, weigh your own setting honestly, and take counsel from mature believers who know it.

Matthew 10:16 (WEB)
“Behold, I send you out as sheep among wolves. Therefore be wise as serpents and harmless as doves.”

We Keep No Lists of People

We do not build central databases of who is involved or where they are — both for the sake of privacy and for the safety of believers in hard places. Whatever is most dangerous to gather is what we choose not to gather. Introductions happen locally, through people you already trust, not through any system at a distance.

Share Only What Is Wise

When you reach out, share only what is safe to share where you are. For sensitive conversations you can create a fresh, separate account — for example an encrypted email or an anonymous messenger — kept apart from your everyday identity. Guarding this information is not fear; it is love for the people who could be affected.

Know and Honor the Laws Where You Are

Laws about religious life and speech differ greatly from one place to another, and some carry real consequences. Learn the laws of your own context, honor them, and take local counsel before you act. No one far away can judge what is wise for your situation — the people who love God and know your setting are your best guides, under His leading.

A Note on Integrity

This work moves through real people and true testimony — never through pretense, fabricated identities, or manufactured attention. We would rather grow slowly and honestly than quickly and falsely. Honesty guards both the good news itself and the people who carry it; a movement built on truth has nothing to hide and nothing that can be exposed.

1 John 1:7 (WEB)
“But if we walk in the light as he is in the light, we have fellowship with one another.”

Before You Act

No one here can tell you what level of risk is appropriate in your situation. Please seek the Lord, consider local realities, and take counsel from mature believers around you.
