Padma Pulse A weekly prayer guide for Bangladesh

Pray for Bangladesh

Padma is what Bangladesh calls the Ganges. Padma Pulse follows its waters, and its sister rivers, from the north to the sea: one region a day from Monday to Saturday, and the whole country on Sunday.

  • Monday: Rangpur
  • Tuesday: Rajshahi
  • Wednesday: Dhaka and Mymensingh
  • Thursday: Sylhet
  • Friday: Chattogram
  • Saturday: Khulna and Barishal
  • Sunday: the whole country, worship only

Each day begins with worship. We adore one side of who God is, pray a passage of Scripture aloud, thank Him and give ourselves back to Him. Then we pray for the region's week: its news, a people group who have not yet heard of Jesus, the church, and the believers who stand firm there.

Prayer only. Padma Pulse never carries news about any organization and never asks for money. Your address is used for this guide and nothing else, and you can leave from any email.

Choose how you would like to receive it

Please share Padma Pulse by email or on paper with people who will pray, not on a website or social media. It never names a believer or places one closer than their division.

Sending it to others

If you lead a team, a church or an organization, forward any Padma Pulse email to the people you pray with, or send them this page so they can sign up themselves.

What happens next

We'll send you one email to confirm. Nothing else arrives until you click the link inside it. This page doesn't track you, and the emails don't either.
