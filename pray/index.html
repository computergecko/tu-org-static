Secure Contact

Pray with us

Prayer starts with worship. Before we ask God for anything, we come to know Him and love Him. Then we carry the people He loves to Him.

That is what these emails are for. Choose what you would like to receive, and we will send it to your inbox. Each one is a short guide to help you worship first, then pray with understanding.

Choose what you would like to receive

The guides name believers only as they wished to be named, and some of the data in them may not be republished. Please share them by email or on paper, never on a website or social media.

What happens next

We'll send you one email to confirm. Nothing else arrives until you click the link inside it. You can change your choices or leave at any time from the link at the bottom of every email.

Privacy

This page doesn't track you, and our emails don't either. We use your address only to send what you chose, and we never share it.

Prefer your phone?

You can also follow along for general prayer updates on WhatsApp or Telegram. These carry general updates only. The prayer guides come by email, to keep them private.
