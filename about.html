How This All Fits Together

You may come across more than one address for this work. They are not competing sites; each simply carries a different part.

  • tsunamiunleashed.org — this site: the heart and the “why.” Why loving Him comes first, and what the Lord seems to be doing.
  • tsunamiunleashed.com — the materials to use and share: the actual teaching, in a form you can take, translate, and pass on.
  • 7gc.me — short links to resources: the updated version, the translation template, and more.

Everything on all three is released freely, under CC0, so it can be copied, mirrored, and translated without asking anyone’s permission.
